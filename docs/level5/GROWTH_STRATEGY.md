# Level 5 Growth Strategy

Level 5 is focused on scaling from MVP to a larger validation cohort.

## Target

Onboard at least 50 real Stellar Testnet users with signed ProofDesk activity.

## Channels

- Bootcamp peers.
- Student communities.
- Certificate/course communities.
- Founder and builder groups.
- Friends who can test a public Testnet wallet flow.

## Retention Loop

1. Tester creates a proof.
2. Tester submits feedback.
3. Feedback is grouped into themes.
4. One improvement is shipped.
5. The next tester batch validates whether the flow is clearer.
