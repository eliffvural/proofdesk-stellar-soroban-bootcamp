# Level 5 Google Form Field Map

The Google Form must collect the exact user onboarding fields requested by Level 5.

| Required item | Google Form question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Wallet address | Paste the Stellar Testnet wallet address you used. | Short answer |
| Email | What email can we use for follow-up? | Short answer |
| Name | What is your name? | Short answer |
| Product feedback | What worked, what was confusing, and what should improve? | Paragraph |
| Product rating | Rate ProofDesk from 1 to 5. | Linear scale |
| Transaction proof | Paste your transaction hash or Stellar explorer URL. | Short answer |

## Export Rule

After collection, export responses to Excel and update:
