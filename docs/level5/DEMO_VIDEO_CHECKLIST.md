# Level 5 Demo Video Checklist

The demo video must show the full product walkthrough and real use cases.

## Required Scenes

1. Open the live deployed application.
2. Show Freighter on Stellar Testnet.
3. Connect wallet.
4. Generate a local document fingerprint.
5. Create a proof and approve the signed transaction.
6. Show transaction hash or Stellar explorer link.
7. Verify the proof.
8. Revoke the proof.
9. Show Level 5 growth validation and evidence export.
10. Show Google Form and Excel feedback workbook.
11. Show pitch deck and roadmap.

## Real Use Cases To Mention

- Certificate verification.
- Agreement timestamping.
- Invoice proof.
- Audit trail evidence.
