# Level 5 User Cohort Evidence

Level 5 requires at least 50 real Testnet users. Do not count demo rows, internal test rows, or read-only verification attempts.

## A User Counts Only If

- The tester submitted their name or alias.
- The tester submitted an email address.
- The tester submitted a public Stellar Testnet wallet address.
- The tester signed at least one Testnet transaction in ProofDesk.
- The tester submitted a transaction hash or Stellar explorer URL.
- The tester rated the product from 1 to 5.
- The tester wrote product feedback.

## Cohort Batches

Collect users in batches of 10:

1. Batch 1: users 1-10
2. Batch 2: users 11-20
3. Batch 3: users 21-30
4. Batch 4: users 31-40
5. Batch 5: users 41-50+

After each batch, export responses and summarize the top feedback theme.
